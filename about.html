Biography

About Aditya Seth

Aditya Seth is a software engineer, cloud architect, and AI researcher from Kolkata, West Bengal, India. He currently serves as the Software, Strategy & AI Initiatives Lead at Techno India Group and as a Research Engineer at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Roorkee.

He holds 8+ cloud certifications including the Google Associate Cloud Engineer (ACE), Microsoft Azure (DP-100, AZ-900, AI-900, SC-900), and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (three separate certifications). He is recognized as a Knight on LeetCode, placing him in the top 2.6% of competitive programmers globally (rank 16,572 out of 645,998), and holds a 6-Star badge on HackerRank.

Aditya received the Chancellor's Gold Medal from Techno India University at Convocation 2024 for outstanding contributions to the Techno Billion AI project - an enterprise AI infrastructure platform built on Google Cloud Platform, Kubernetes, and Prometheus/Grafana. He also achieved GATE 2026 DS&AI: AIR 7,526, placing him in the top 5% nationally.

As a community leader, he served as Ex-Lead Organizer of GDG On-Campus Techno Main Salt Lake (growing the chapter to 1,950+ members, earning Gold Tier from Google) and as a Gold Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador (top 5% globally). He co-founded Microsoft Aurora Kolkata and has mentored over 2,800 students in cloud and software engineering.

His engineering work spans distributed systems, ML/AI pipelines, and cloud-native infrastructure. Notable projects include EduCRM (enterprise SaaS managing 30,000+ users and 25,000 concurrent AI conversations), AgentHosp (40-step autonomous multi-agent clinical simulation with 16 AI agents), and ShieldK8s (Kubernetes runtime threat monitoring with ELK Stack and Prometheus).
